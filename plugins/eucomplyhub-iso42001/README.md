# ISO/IEC 42001 AI Management System (eucomplyhub)

A community-contributed VerifyWise plugin implementing **ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System (AIMS)** as a starter framework for mid-market SaaS preparing for the **August 2, 2026** EU AI Act Article 50 enforcement deadline.

Maintained by [eucomplyhub.com](https://eucomplyhub.com) — EU AI Act + ISO 42001 audit specialist for mid-market SaaS (€5-15M ARR). Triple-framework methodology aligns ISO 42001 with EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF.

## What this plugin covers

**Management clauses (4-10):**
- Clause 4 — Context of the Organization (AI roles, regulatory exposure, AIMS scope)
- Clause 5 — Leadership (AI policy, governance roles including Article 14 oversight)
- Clause 6 — Planning (AI risk assessment + treatment + impact assessment / FRIA)
- Clause 7 — Support (Article 4 AI literacy, Annex IV documentation)
- Clause 8 — Operation (vendor management, post-market monitoring)
- Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation (Article 15 + Article 50 metrics)
- Clause 10 — Improvement (CAPA, continual improvement)

**Selected Annex A controls** mapped to EU AI Act articles:
- A.2.2 AI policy
- A.3.2 AI roles and responsibilities
- A.6.2.2 AI impact assessment
- A.7.4 Data quality (↔ Article 10)
- A.8.2 System and computing resources documentation
- A.9.2 Transparency (↔ Article 50)
- A.9.3 External reporting (↔ Article 73 incident reporting)
- A.10.2 Human oversight (↔ Article 14)

## Triple-framework alignment

This plugin is designed to be used alongside (not as a replacement for):
- VerifyWise's NIST AI RMF coverage
- An EU AI Act compliance framework (community contribution candidate)

For mid-market SaaS facing all three regimes simultaneously, eucomplyhub recommends a single mapped audit rather than three parallel projects. See [eucomplyhub.com/iso42001](https://eucomplyhub.com/iso42001) for the triple-framework crosswalk reference.

## What this plugin is NOT

- Not a full ISO 42001 certification preparation (24+ months engagement)
- Not a replacement for a competent AIMS lead auditor
- Not legal advice
- Not all 38 Annex A controls (representative subset; full SoA covers all)

For a comprehensive ISO 42001 cert readiness audit (Tier 3, €15,997) or boxed mid-market audit (Audit-in-a-Box™, €3,997 founding rate), see [eucomplyhub.com/audit](https://eucomplyhub.com/audit).

## Installation

This plugin is auto-imported when installed. The framework structure becomes available in your VerifyWise instance immediately.
